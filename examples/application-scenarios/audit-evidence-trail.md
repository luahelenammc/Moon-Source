# Transparency & audit — evidence trail

> **Status:** hypothetical / fictional didactic scenario. This example illustrates how Moon Source could be applied to a common context problem. It is not evidence of external adoption, implementation, measured impact or independent validation.

## Common problem

A fictional team can describe its decisions but cannot easily reconstruct which source supported each one. Versions are duplicated, reports collapse fact and interpretation, and a later reviewer cannot tell what changed or what was merely inferred.

## Why raw “more context” is insufficient

More documents do not create provenance. A report can grow while its claims become less traceable. A citation without source jurisdiction, date or transformation history may look precise and still be unable to support the conclusion.

## Moon Source reading of the field

Use Evidence & Claims to set the ceiling for each public statement. Use Signal Calibration to distinguish observation, convergence, working inference and meaningful alternatives. Use Credits & Attribution Ops when authorship, transformation or lineage matters. Use Source Hygiene to remove duplicate or superseded evidence without erasing recoverable history.

## Relevant components and portables

- [Evidence & Claims](../../EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md) for claim ceilings.
- [Signal Calibration](../../docs/SIGNAL_CALIBRATION.md) for bounded interpretation.
- [Credits & Attribution Ops](../../docs/CREDITS_ATTRIBUTION_OPS.md) for lineage and custody.
- [Source Hygiene](../../docs/SOURCE_HYGIENE.md) for provenance and version diagnosis.
- [MSL 4.3](../../portables/msl/MSL_4_3.md) for a compact evidence ledger.

## Possible smallest materialization

Create an evidence ledger with claim, source, source date, transformation, evidence class, responsible reviewer, current status and claim ceiling. Keep raw evidence and restricted material in their governing systems; the ledger carries enough provenance for a bounded audit.

## Authority and update rule

The source artifact governs the fact it contains. The ledger records how a claim was derived and may not silently strengthen it. A material source change reopens dependent claims. Attribution and transformation records travel with derivatives, while private or third-party details remain bounded.

## Validation and readback

For each claim, ask whether the source supports the exact wording, whether inference is labeled, whether a meaningful alternative remains and whether the current version is the one reviewed. Read back the published report against the ledger rather than trusting a manually copied summary.

## What this scenario does not claim

It does not provide an audit result, compliance certification, legal conclusion or measured transparency improvement. It is a fictional evidence-trail pattern.
