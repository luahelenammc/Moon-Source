# Third sector — network continuity through turnover

> **Status:** hypothetical / fictional didactic scenario. This example illustrates how Moon Source could be applied to a common context problem. It is not evidence of external adoption, implementation, measured impact or independent validation.

## Common problem

A fictional small initiative coordinates volunteers, partners and recurring activities. Decisions live in personal messages, turnover causes institutional memory loss and reporting requires traceability without creating a second bureaucracy.

## Why raw “more context” is insufficient

Copying every message into a shared archive creates administrative weight without clarifying what remains active. A new volunteer still cannot tell which decision is current, who owns a relationship or what can be shared publicly.

## Moon Source reading of the field

Use Field to Form to materialize only what the network repeatedly needs. Use Responsibility Map to distinguish partner, activity, source and handoff ownership. Use MSL 4.3 for continuity packets and archive boundaries. Use Credits & Attribution Ops when partner material, authorship or permission travels with a report.

## Relevant components and portables

- [Field to Form](../../docs/FIELD_TO_FORM.md) for proportional materialization.
- [Responsibility Map](../../docs/RESPONSIBILITY_MAP.md) for network ownership.
- [Credits & Attribution Ops](../../docs/CREDITS_ATTRIBUTION_OPS.md) for lineage and permissions.
- [MSL 4.3](../../portables/msl/MSL_4_3.md) for packets, handoffs and archives.
- [Source Hygiene](../../docs/SOURCE_HYGIENE.md) for bounded review as the corpus grows.

## Possible smallest materialization

Create one living network ledger with current partners, recurring commitments, next contact, responsible person, public/private boundary and last verified update. Add a short handoff packet only when responsibility changes; archive old decisions without making them active instructions.

## Authority and update rule

The person or group responsible for a relationship updates its current state. A volunteer can contribute evidence but does not silently change a partner commitment. Materialize only recurring or costly context, and review the ledger when turnover, a commitment change or a reporting cycle occurs.

## Validation and readback

Ask whether a new volunteer can recover the next action, whether each entry has an owner and whether reporting can trace a claim without exposing private messages. Read back the living ledger against the network before each material reporting cycle.

## What this scenario does not claim

It does not describe a real nonprofit, partnership, volunteer network or reporting result. It illustrates proportional continuity under limited administrative capacity.
