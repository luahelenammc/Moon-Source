# People & learning — onboarding and capability continuity

> **Status:** hypothetical / fictional didactic scenario. This example illustrates how Moon Source could be applied to a common context problem. It is not evidence of external adoption, implementation, measured impact or independent validation.

## Common problem

A fictional team trains newcomers through scattered slides, messages and informal explanations from experienced colleagues. The AI gives generic answers because role, local language, limits and current practice are missing.

## Why raw “more context” is insufficient

A large training archive does not tell a learner what applies to their role or which instruction is current. It may also preserve obsolete practice and expose material that the learner is not meant to use. Context needs routing, not just volume.

## Moon Source reading of the field

Use Setup 3.0 to infer the learner's actual need and maturity. Use Field to Form to choose a compact onboarding surface. Use Procedural Projection when a stable method should become a reusable procedure, while keeping the governing source distinct. Preflight handles questions that are ambiguous, sensitive or likely to route to the wrong material.

## Possible smallest materialization

Create a role-specific onboarding packet with current vocabulary, first tasks, authority sources, escalation routes, known limits, examples of acceptable outputs and an update owner. Link to deeper procedures instead of reproducing the entire corpus.

## Authority and update rule

The process owner governs the current procedure. A learner may suggest a correction, but the owner approves changes. When practice changes, update the source and packet together or mark the packet stale; do not let the AI silently normalize conflicting instructions.

## Validation and readback

Check whether the packet answers the learner's first real questions, respects role boundaries and points to current sources. Read back after each material process change and retire obsolete examples without losing their lineage when it matters.

## What this scenario does not claim

It does not claim improved training, retention, autonomy or workforce outcomes. It is a fictional onboarding design example.
