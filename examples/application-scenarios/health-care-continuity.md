# Health & care — multi-team continuity

> **Status:** hypothetical / fictional didactic scenario. This example illustrates how Moon Source could be applied to a common context problem. It is not evidence of external adoption, implementation, measured impact or independent validation.

## Common problem

A fictional care service has sensitive information distributed across team notes, handoffs and separate source systems. Several professionals need different facets of the same current context, while authority to interpret, edit or disclose each facet is not identical.

Older notes remain visible after the situation changes. A longer shared chat makes retrieval harder and does not decide which source governs the current status.

## Why raw “more context” is insufficient

Accumulating every note can reproduce stale or unnecessary sensitive material. It also blurs source authority, audience boundaries and update responsibility. Reachable information is not automatically current, relevant or authorized for every reader.

## Moon Source reading of the field

Use Preflight to identify the actual decision, audience, sensitivity and question threshold before loading material. Use the Responsibility Map to distinguish care facts, current status, handoff responsibility and communication scope. Use Connected Sources only with explicit substrate, jurisdiction, freshness and readback rules. Use Source Hygiene when duplicated or superseded notes compete.

## Possible smallest materialization

Create a bounded continuity packet containing only the current question, current-status source, relevant facts, authority by facet, freshness date, disclosure boundary, open uncertainty and next handoff. Keep the underlying clinical or operational systems authoritative; the packet transports context and does not become a new source of truth.

## Authority and update rule

The source responsible for each facet governs it. A current-status source supersedes older summaries when the two conflict, while older material remains recoverable as history. Only an authorized role may mutate the governing source. Every update records what changed, why, source freshness and who or what must read it back.

## Validation and readback

Before use, verify that the packet contains no unnecessary sensitive material, that each claim has a source, that the current status is not stale, and that the receiving professional can identify the governing source and next action. Read back the rendered packet against the source after material changes.

## What this scenario does not claim

It does not describe a real service, patient, professional, clinical outcome, safety result or external implementation. It illustrates a public reasoning and materialization contract only.
