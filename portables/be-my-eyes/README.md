# 👁️ Be My Eyes

**Read a message, thread, screenshot, note or draft as a human scene — without pretending inference is fact.**

[▶️ Start here](BE_MY_EYES.md#first-use) · [⬇️ Download package](../../downloads/be-my-eyes-1.0.zip) · [🌙 Moon Source](../../README.md)

## Use this when

- the literal words are not the whole interaction;
- you want to map relationship, power, plausible subtext or likely reception;
- you want to check whether you may be overreading a cue;
- you need the right response posture before drafting the response itself.

## What it does

Be My Eyes separates observation from relational inference and unknowns, supports forward reads of received material and inverse reads of how outgoing material may land, and keeps overread visible instead of hiding it behind certainty.

## What it does not do

It does not read minds, establish motive, diagnose people or replace findings of fact in high-stakes settings. It organizes the communication scene and calibrates interpretation.
