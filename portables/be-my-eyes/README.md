# Be My Eyes portable

Be My Eyes is the standalone Moon Source portable for **contextual scene reading**: helping an AI understand human communication as a relational scene rather than reducing it to literal wording.

## First use

Use Be My Eyes when the material is a message, thread, screenshot, meeting note, post, draft, described scene or other interaction where relationship, subtext, overread, likely reception or response posture matters. It is a method portable, not installed vision, mind-reading or a promise about another person's reaction.

For repository-wide orientation, return to [Moon Source first use](../../README.md#first-use).

### Nothing is installed

Reading or pasting [`BE_MY_EYES.md`](BE_MY_EYES.md) does not create visual access, retrieve private context or diagnose anyone. If the current AI surface cannot see an image or interface, attach the screenshot, paste the thread, transcribe the audio or describe the missing details.

### Say this first

For a received or observed scene:

```text
Use Be My Eyes to read this scene.
Separate what is observed, what is inferred, the plausible subtext,
the risk of overreading, and what response posture would fit.

Scene: [paste, attach or describe the material]
```

For an outgoing message or action:

```text
Use Be My Eyes for an inverse read.
Tell me how this may land, what is clear, what may be overread,
and what response posture the draft invites.

Draft: [paste the message or describe the action]
```

### What happens next

The AI should reconstruct the scene, identify actors and relationship where supported, distinguish observation from inference, keep plausible subtext separate from fact, note unknowns and offer a proportionate response axis before polishing words.

The portable supports both **forward reading** of received or observed material and **inverse reading** of how outgoing material may land. If several interpretations remain live, keep them separate instead of collapsing uncertainty into a motive claim.

### Minimal example

You received: “Sure, send it over when you get a chance.” Ask for a forward read and include the surrounding thread if it matters. A useful result distinguishes the literal acceptance, the presence or absence of urgency, the relationship context and the risk of inventing irritation that the sentence alone does not prove.

For an inverse read, provide a draft and ask whether it sounds clear, cold, defensive, warm or over-eager in the actual relationship, with evidence and uncertainty named.

### Manual actions and visual fallback

You may need to attach the screenshot, paste the thread, transcribe audio or describe missing visual details. Be My Eyes cannot retrieve private context that the current surface cannot access and cannot send a response for you unless a separate authorized action exists.

In a high-stakes situation, treat the reading as a working aid and seek stronger evidence or qualified help. Subtext is a hypothesis, not mind-reading.

### What this portable does not claim

- No access to hidden motives or internal states.
- No psychological diagnosis or certainty about another person's mind.
- No guaranteed prediction of reception.
- No permission to treat inference as observation.
- No substitute for evidence, consent or professional judgment where those matter.

### Troubleshooting

If the interpretation feels too certain, say:

```text
Redo this with four labels: observed, inferred, working hypothesis, unknown.
List the evidence for each inference and identify any likely overread.
```

If the AI lacks the visual or conversational scene, provide the smallest missing excerpt instead of asking it to invent context.

## Use it when

You want an AI to read a message, thread, screenshot, meeting note, post, draft or described interaction for:

- what is materially happening;
- actors, relationship and power structure;
- plausible subtext;
- risk and opportunity;
- overread;
- likely reception;
- the best response posture before wording.

The portable supports both **forward reading** of received/observed material and **inverse reading** of how outgoing material may land.

## Boundary

Be My Eyes separates observation from inference and preserves uncertainty. It is not mind-reading, psychological diagnosis, guaranteed reaction prediction or a substitute for stronger evidentiary standards in high-stakes domains.
