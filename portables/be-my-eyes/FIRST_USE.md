# Be My Eyes — First-Use Guide

_Read this before using the Be My Eyes portable._

## What this is for

Be My Eyes helps an AI read a message, thread, screenshot, meeting note, post, draft or described interaction as a human scene. It keeps visible observation separate from inference, plausible subtext, working hypotheses and unknowns.

It supports two directions:

- **Forward reading:** what a received or observed scene may mean and how it may be received.
- **Inverse reading:** how an outgoing message or action may land before you send it.

## Nothing is installed

Reading `BE_MY_EYES.md` does not create vision access, mind-reading, psychological diagnosis or guaranteed reaction prediction. If an image or interface is not available to the AI, paste it, attach it or describe the relevant details.

Use the canonical body [BE_MY_EYES.md](BE_MY_EYES.md). This [root FIRST_USE.md](../../FIRST_USE.md) explains the first run and does not override the semantic body.

## Say this first

For a received or observed scene:

~~~text
Use Be My Eyes to read this scene.
Separate what is observed, what is inferred, the plausible subtext,
the risk of overreading, and what response posture would fit.

Scene: [paste, attach or describe the material]
~~~

For an outgoing message:

~~~text
Use Be My Eyes for an inverse read.
Tell me how this may land, what is clear, what may be overread,
and what response posture the draft invites.

Draft: [paste the message]
~~~

## What happens next

The AI should reconstruct the scene, identify actors and relationship where supported, distinguish evidence from inference, note unknowns and offer a proportionate response axis before polishing words.

A plausible interpretation is not a fact. If several readings remain live, keep them separate instead of collapsing uncertainty into a motive claim.

## Minimal example

You received: “Sure, send it over when you get a chance.”

Provide the surrounding thread if available and ask for a forward read. The useful output distinguishes the literal acceptance, the absence or presence of urgency, the relationship context and the risk of inventing irritation that the message alone does not prove.

For an inverse read, provide a draft and ask whether it sounds clear, cold, defensive, warm or over-eager in the actual relationship.

## Manual actions and unavailable capabilities

You may need to attach the screenshot, paste the thread, transcribe audio or describe missing visual details. Be My Eyes cannot retrieve private context that the current surface cannot access and cannot send the response for you unless a separate authorized action exists.

In a high-stakes situation, treat the reading as a working aid and seek stronger evidence or qualified help.

## What this portable does not claim

- No mind-reading or access to hidden motives.
- No psychological diagnosis or certainty about another person's internal state.
- No guaranteed prediction of reception.
- No permission to treat inference as observation.
- No substitute for evidence, consent or professional judgment where those matter.

## Troubleshooting

If the interpretation feels too certain, say:

~~~text
Redo this with four labels: observed, inferred, working hypothesis, unknown.
List the evidence for each inference and identify any likely overread.
~~~

If the AI lacks the visual or conversational scene, provide the smallest missing excerpt instead of asking it to invent context.
