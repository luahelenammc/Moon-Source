# Public skill promotion decisions

These are portable projections for the public repository, not evidence of installation or active runtime use. Each candidate was admitted on its registry-defined recurrent job and distinct canonical responsibility. The bundled canonical method supplies detailed branching, QA and boundaries. Decision is **build candidate**; archive/structural tests do not establish semantic sufficiency or real-world performance. Independent use tests and review remain necessary before stronger claims.
