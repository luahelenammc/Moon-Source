## 1. Two different questions

Moon Source separates **operational use**, **legal reuse rights** and **provenance governance**.

### Operational use

You may read and apply Moon Source as a reference architecture for context work: use its concepts, diagnostic questions, public procedures and declared interfaces to structure your own work with people, projects, teams or AI systems.

Typical uses include:

- creating or repairing a governed context source;
- deciding which source owns which claim or state;
- designing handoffs, packets, registries, bridges, archives or other proportional forms;
- using MSL as a structural grammar;
- using public portables in the scope they declare;
- teaching or discussing the public architecture with clear attribution;
- studying Moon Source as a documented method or implementation lineage.

### Reuse, modification and redistribution

The public Moon Source body is openly reusable under a mixed standard-license model. Software, executable code, automation and technical implementation use Apache-2.0. Documentation, methods, public portables, prose, diagrams and textual knowledge artifacts use CC-BY-4.0 unless a file-level or third-party notice says otherwise.

The complete routing and standard texts live in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md), [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), [`NOTICE`](NOTICE), [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES/) and [file-level SPDX metadata](REUSE.toml). Those standard licenses govern copying, modification, adaptation, publication, redistribution, forks, mirrors and commercial use within the rights Moon is authorized to grant.

This document does not create extra restrictions or replace the applicable standard license. Public availability still does not relicense third-party material, disclose private corpora or turn abstract ideas and methods into universally copyright-exclusive works.

## 3. How to use Moon Source responsibly

When using the public method as a reference architecture:

1. Start from the problem or field, not from a desire to reproduce every Moon Source artifact.
2. Use only the components that fit the actual responsibility.
3. Keep your own sources, authority and local decisions distinct from Moon Source's canonical body.
4. Mark local adaptations as local adaptations.
5. Preserve material third-party lineage when Moon Source itself points to an external donor or influence.
6. Do not present your implementation as an official Moon Source implementation unless that relationship actually exists.
7. Do not imply endorsement, partnership, validation, adoption or certification by Moon merely because the method was used.
8. Preserve uncertainty where permission, authorship or lineage is unresolved.

## 4. Use classes

| Use | What it means | Minimum attribution behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Reference** | Concepts or public architecture inform your own work | Name Moon Source and link the canonical repository when practical |
| **Teaching / discussion** | You explain, compare or teach the public method | Preserve creator credit and identify Moon Source as the source method |
| **Permitted adaptation** | A component is modified for a new context under applicable permission | Name Moon Source, identify the original component and label your changes |
| **Permitted fork** | A public artifact is forked under applicable permission | Preserve canonical origin, version/path and local fork identity |
| **Mirror** | The same artifact is copied to another surface | Preserve canonical path and, when exact identity matters, version/fingerprint |
| **Composite output** | Moon Source materially contributes alongside other sources | Credit Moon Source and the other material lineages without implying endorsement |
| **Generated output** | An AI or workflow uses Moon Source components to produce a new artifact | Credit the material components when their contribution is material and the output is meant for reuse or publication |

These are public provenance classes, not substitute legal classifications.

## 6. Adaptation rule

When you share or adapt a CC-BY-4.0-covered Moon Source component:

- keep the original component name and canonical path recoverable;
- identify your local project or adaptation;
- state what changed when the difference matters;
- do not attribute your local changes to Moon;
- do not erase external lineage already recorded by the source;
- do not imply that Moon reviewed, endorsed or validated the adaptation unless she did.
- indicate whether changes were made and link the applicable license;
- satisfy Apache-2.0 notice requirements instead when the material is software or automation.

Recommended compact pattern:

> Adapted from **[Moon Source component]** by Lua Helena Moon Martins Cardoso (Moon), with AI-assisted coauthorial development by Áurion. Local adaptation by **[person / project]**, **[version/date]**. Original: **[canonical path]**. Licensed under **CC BY 4.0**.

## 7. Mirrors and transport

A convenience mirror does not become semantic authority merely because it is easier to access.

For exact mirrors:

- preserve the canonical repository path;
- preserve version or release identity when applicable;
- preserve fingerprint when exact byte identity is promised;
- label stale or transformed copies honestly;
- route disagreements back to the canonical repository.

## 8. Third-party lineage

Moon Source may contain public references to external ideas, projects, people, communities or donor lineages. Moon Source attribution does not erase those origins.

When a reused component carries material third-party lineage, preserve it proportionally. Use [Credits & Attribution Ops](docs/CREDITS_ATTRIBUTION_OPS.md) when the asset's origin, custody, canonical identity, transformation history, permission envelope, disclosure boundary, derivative lineage or evidentiary record needs to be governed in detail.

## 9. Claim ceiling

Using Moon Source does not establish that:

- Moon endorsed the user or implementation;
- the implementation is official;
- an organization adopted Moon Source;
- a result was validated by Moon;
- a method is universally effective;
- a component is licensed beyond its actual terms;
- private Moon Source, Local Moon Source, Citadel or other internal corpora are included in the public release.
