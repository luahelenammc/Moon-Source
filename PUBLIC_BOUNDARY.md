# Moon Source · Data Protection and Claim Boundaries

Moon Source documents reusable context methods and public reference implementations. This page summarizes the permissions and factual limits relevant to their use.

## Data and permission

Only retrieve, process, transport or persist data that the person or organization has authorized for the task. Keep credentials, health and client records, identifiable personal information and confidential account context in appropriate user-controlled systems. Access to a connector never creates authority to disclose its content.

Use source references with their actual license and attribution requirements. Do not infer ownership from availability. When instructions, data and external sources disagree, identify which source has authority for the specific action.

## Evidence and use

Public documentation, synthetic examples, bounded tests, actual deployments and measured outcomes are distinct evidence levels. The fictional [application examples](examples/application-scenarios/README.md) demonstrate possible workflows, not documented client results.

Where a task involves clinical, legal, financial or safety-critical decisions, follow appropriate professional or institutional review.
