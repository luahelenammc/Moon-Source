# 🎚️ Signal Calibration — Moon Source component

A bounded method for using weak, convergent or ambiguous evidence without turning inference into fact or uncertainty into blindness.

> **Calibrate confidence; do not erase signal.**

## Function

Use Signal Calibration when a field contains several small, incomplete or ambiguous cues and a useful working interpretation is needed. The method sits between observation and established claim: it preserves evidence, authority and uncertainty while allowing the strongest defensible inference to remain visible.

It does not make weak evidence decisive. It prevents “not fully proven” from being treated as either “already established” or “epistemically irrelevant”.

**MSL profile:** 5.1-compatible Markdown-native method surface; preserve observation/inference distinctions and uncertainty boundaries.

## The two symmetric failures

| Failure | What goes wrong |
|---|---|
| **Certainty inflation** | Weak or ambiguous cues are promoted to fact, motive, diagnosis, causality or public accusation. |
| **Epistemic sterilization** | A plausible interpretation supported by multiple cues is flattened into “we cannot know”, a decorative list of possibilities or caveats so aggressive that no useful judgment remains. |

The method avoids both by making the level of support explicit and proportionate to the consequence of being wrong.

## Responsibility distinctions

| Term | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Observation** | Material directly available: behavior, source content, measurement, event or stated fact. |
| **Signal** | A cue that may carry meaning but is insufficient by itself. |
| **Convergence** | Several relevant cues supporting the same interpretation. Repeated or correlated cues are not automatically independent evidence. |
| **Working inference** | The best current interpretation supported by the field, kept explicitly distinct from established fact. |
| **Alternative hypothesis** | A materially plausible competing explanation that could change the judgment. |
| **Established claim** | A statement presented as fact and therefore subject to the relevant evidence and authority standard. |

The distinction between behavior and internal state matters. A behavioral pattern can sometimes be described before motive, intention or diagnosis can responsibly be claimed.

## Public calibration loop

Use only the depth the field requires. This is a decision loop, not a ritual.

1. **Inventory observable cues.** Start with what is actually present. Record the source or authority of each cue when that matters.
2. **Separate behavior from mind-reading.** Describe what happened before explaining what someone wanted, felt or intended.
3. **Steelman the leading interpretation.** Reconstruct the strongest defensible version of the current read before weakening it with caveats.
4. **Look for convergence and dependency.** Ask which cues genuinely add support and which merely repeat the same source, event or assumption.
5. **Test meaningful alternatives.** Consider one or a few plausible explanations capable of changing the result. Do not build a catalogue of every logically possible explanation.
6. **Calibrate by consequence.** Raise the evidentiary bar as the cost of error rises. A casual working interpretation, an irreversible action, a public accusation, a diagnosis and a legal or medical claim do not share one threshold.
7. **Return a dual output.** State both the epistemic floor — what is actually established — and the working read — what the field currently supports best.
8. **Name the update condition.** State what new evidence would materially strengthen, weaken or reverse the working read.

## Output contract

When the distinction changes the decision, a calibrated answer should make visible:

- what is observed;
- what signals those observations provide;
- what interpretation currently leads;
- why the cues converge, remain dependent or fail to converge;
- which alternative is materially meaningful;
- what is established and what remains inferred;
- the consequence or risk level;
- qualitative confidence, without invented precision;
- what would update the judgment.

The full contract is not mandatory for every low-stakes answer. Structure should be sufficient for the field, not maximal by default.


## Interior–Membrane Split

Signal Calibration can operate across two distinct responsibilities when a high-resolution working analysis must later circulate to another audience or jurisdiction:

> **Exploration and circulation are different responsibilities.**

Activate this split only when a meaningful boundary exists: a useful analysis must travel; local, private or sensitive material cannot travel literally; the consequence of error changes between working context and output; sanitization is already erasing the mechanism; or raw material is at risk of crossing an unsuitable boundary. It is a decision aid, not a mandatory ritual for every case.

### Interior / working layer

The interior (or working) layer is an explicit governed working representation. It may preserve:

- observations, signals, convergence, working inferences, alternatives and counterevidence;
- local or provisional language when it increases analytic resolution;
- mechanisms, asymmetries, contradictions and consequences;
- explicit epistemic labels and qualitative confidence;
- a real `no_new_inference_with_reason` result when the field adds no new understanding.

It does not convert inference into fact, and it does not invent novelty merely to satisfy a format or a quota.

### Membrane / boundary layer

The membrane (or boundary) layer translates and gates circulation. It decides what can cross, to which destination, under which authority and with what consequence:

- resolve audience, purpose, destination, authority, exposure and consequence;
- remove or abstract private, local, personal or irrelevant dependencies;
- preserve the original epistemic status, or make it more conservative; translation is not promotion;
- translate a surviving mechanism into observable behavior, process, risk, ownership, rule or action when that survives the gate;
- retain material that cannot cross without distortion, returning `no_exportable_delta`.

**Sanitization is not causal amnesia.**

**Sanitize dependencies, not the mechanism.**

A boundary may narrow circulation without retroactively narrowing the working field that produced the analysis. If removing private dependencies also removes the mechanism, keep the material local rather than exporting a hollow summary.

### Minimal split output

No literal YAML format is required. When the split is material, make the working and boundary decisions visible enough to audit:

- **working layer:** established material, working inference, mechanism, alternatives or counterevidence, and qualitative confidence;
- **boundary layer:** destination, exportable mechanism, translated output, retained or withheld material, epistemic status after translation, and `exportable_delta` or `no_exportable_delta`.

### Boundary QA

- **Causal retention test:** after translation, can the recipient still understand why the conclusion matters?
- **Epistemic conservation test:** did anything cross with stronger epistemic status than it had inside?
- **Dependency test:** which elements are load-bearing mechanism and which are merely local or private dependencies?
- **Destination test:** would the same output be appropriate for a private working note, an internal team memo and a public artifact? If yes by default, the boundary is probably under-modelled.

If no material distinction in destination or consequence exists, use ordinary calibration rather than manufacturing a two-layer ritual.

### Synthetic example (non-evidentiary)

A team notices recurring delays concentrated at one approval point.

- **Interior:** the pattern is recorded; centralization or single-point dependency is a leading working inference; a formal regulatory requirement remains a meaningful alternative.
- **Membrane:** a broad report does not assert a person's motive. It preserves the mechanism — a single approval dependency creates delay risk — and recommends backup ownership or a review of the approval path. The human cause remains unestablished.

This example is synthetic and does not report a real organization, adoption or outcome.

## Generic example

A team lead repeatedly asks to be copied on operational decisions and follows up when excluded.

- **Observed:** repeated requests for inclusion and follow-up after exclusion.
- **Signal:** a pattern of information-control or centralization behavior may be present.
- **Working inference:** the person likely wants to retain oversight of the flow. If further cues converge, a concern about credit or territoriality may become a useful working read.
- **Alternative:** the role may require visibility for accountability, safety or coordination.
- **Established floor:** the inclusion pattern is observed; the person's motive is not established merely by that pattern.
- **Update condition:** evidence about the formal role, how the person treats comparable decisions and what happens after inclusion would strengthen or weaken the interpretation.

The example permits a useful read without converting motive into fact or treating the alternative as equally supported merely because it is possible.

## 🛠️ Apply it now

Copy this bounded worksheet into an AI conversation:

~~~text
Run Moon Source Signal Calibration on the material below.

Return:
- observable cues;
- source or authority of each cue when relevant;
- the leading working inference;
- why the cues converge, remain dependent or do not converge;
- the strongest meaningful alternative hypothesis;
- what is established;
- what remains inferred;
- consequence / risk level;
- current confidence in qualitative language only;
- what evidence would materially update the judgment.

Keep behavior separate from claimed motive, intention, diagnosis or causality.
Do not invent scores, thresholds or probabilities.
Do not present a public accusation, diagnosis or legal/medical conclusion from weak signals alone.
Do not erase a supported working inference solely because certainty is unavailable.
Raise the evidentiary bar when the consequence of error is high.

Material:
[describe or attach the field]
~~~

## Failure modes and QA

Check for:

- caveat-first flattening;
- mind-reading presented as observation;
- false symmetry between a well-supported interpretation and a merely possible alternative;
- possibility dumping;
- confidence stronger than the evidence;
- ignoring convergent weak signals because no single cue is decisive;
- treating correlated repetitions as independent evidence;
- using low-stakes inference standards for high-stakes accusations or irreversible action;
- invented precision, scores or probabilities.

### Boundary failures

- **Premature sanitization** — reducing the working field before the mechanism is understood;
- **Causal amnesia** — producing a safer-sounding output that removes the operational relation that made the analysis useful;
- **Epistemic laundering** — allowing a hypothesis to cross as fact or as a stronger claim;
- **Membrane-as-censor** — deleting instead of translating when a public-safe mechanism could survive;
- **Local-dependency leakage** — allowing private, personal or irrelevant dependencies to cross without need;
- **One-output-for-every-destination** — treating a private note, internal memo and public artifact as if they had the same contract;
- **Double authority** — creating a second capability or owner for a responsibility already held by Signal Calibration.

Ask:

1. Did uncertainty change the confidence level, or did it erase the interpretation entirely?
2. Could a reader tell which part is observed and which part is the working inference?
3. Is the alternative capable of changing the judgment, or is it present only to perform balance?
4. Does the consequence of error justify the current evidentiary bar?

## Claim ceiling

This is a bounded qualitative reasoning and calibration method. It is:

- not proof of human motive, intention or internal state;
- not a diagnostic instrument;
- not a scientific validation claim;
- not a hidden scoring engine;
- not a replacement for domain-specific evidence standards;
- not evidence of external adoption or measured impact.
- not access to hidden model reasoning or chain-of-thought, and not a requirement to reveal private deliberation;
- not a system for detecting human intent or an automatic authorization to circulate material;
