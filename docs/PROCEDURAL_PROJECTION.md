# 🧭 Procedural Projection

A public method for turning a stable way of working into a reusable procedural capability without confusing procedure with source authority.

> A **source** governs truth, state and authority. A **procedural projection** governs reusable procedure and quality checks.

This document exposes the responsibility contract and public diagnostic surface. It does not publish the private compiler, promotion gates, evaluation corpus or automatic synchronization machinery.

When a procedure is adapted from another source or transported into a new surface, apply [Credits & Attribution Ops](CREDITS_ATTRIBUTION_OPS.md) so the procedural projection does not lose its lineage, permission scope or local authorship.

## 🔗 Responsibility contract

| Object | Owns | Does not replace |
|---|---|---|
| **Source** | Facts, decisions, rules, current state, provenance and authority | A reusable procedure |
| **Procedure** | A repeatable way of working, including triggers, steps, boundaries and QA | The source of truth for every fact |
| **Skill / procedural projection** | A transportable representation of a procedure that can be invoked or followed | A private corpus or proof of impact |
| **Project** | A bounded domain, purpose, participants and outputs | A generic procedure |
| **Task** | One bounded action or recurring obligation | The project architecture |
| **Mirror / fallback** | Procedural transport when a native skill surface is unavailable | Native execution or semantic authority |

The same Moon Source method may therefore appear as a source, a procedure, a native skill, a portable procedural file or a handoff. The form follows the capability and the available execution surface.

## 🧱 When a procedure earns projection

A recurring method is a candidate for procedural projection when it has:

- a stable responsibility that recurs across cases;
- a clear positive trigger and, where useful, a negative trigger;
- an output contract that another person or AI can inspect;
- hard boundaries, stop lines and uncertainty handling;
- a QA step that checks whether the intended procedure survived transport;
- a source or doctrine that remains authoritative for facts and live state.

Do not freeze live project state into a generic procedure. A procedure may point to the source that supplies current names, dates, permissions or decisions, but it should not silently become that source.

Syntactic validity is not enough. A bundle can parse, install or look complete while losing the operating intelligence that made the original method work.

## 🔄 Source ↔ Skill synchronization

Synchronization here is a governed relationship, not an automatic promise.

- A doctrinal or methodological change in the source may require a projection update.
- A factual or live-state change normally belongs in the source, not in the generic procedure.
- A procedural improvement may flow back into the source when it matures into doctrine.
- Renames, version changes and deprecations must be reflected in mirrors instead of leaving them silently stale.
- The source remains authoritative for truth, state and provenance.
- A mirror, portable or handoff should make its version and freshness legible.

Use [Mirror Synchronization](MIRROR_SYNCHRONIZATION.md) for the repository's concrete public mirror contract. That contract verifies bounded file identity; it does not claim semantic automation across every surface.

## 🧠 Semantic undercompilation

Semantic undercompilation is a public failure mode: a procedural projection is structurally valid but too thin to reconstruct the intended method.

Check for losses in:

- triggers and anti-triggers;
- stop lines and guardrails;
- output shape and acceptance criteria;
- QA and recovery logic;
- source authority and provenance;
- authorship, adaptation relationship and permission scope;
- edge conditions and uncertainty;
- the distinction between current state and reusable doctrine.

The audit question is simple:

> Could a fresh person or AI reconstruct the intended procedure from this projection without inventing the missing operating rules?

Do not infer private thresholds or hidden evaluation suites from this question. It is a reconstructability check, not a benchmark claim.

## 🪞 Native, portable and fallback surfaces

When the environment supports native skill execution, a procedure may be materialized there. When it does not, the same responsibility may travel as a public procedural source, mirror or handoff.

Describe the surface honestly:

- **native capability:** the environment can invoke the procedure as a skill;
- **portable projection:** the procedure is supplied as a readable source;
- **fallback / mirror:** a transport preserves the method while native invocation is unavailable.

A fallback method is useful, but it must not be described as native invocation. Product-specific availability is date-sensitive and should not be turned into a universal law.

## 🛠️ Apply it now

Copy this bounded diagnostic into an AI conversation:

~~~
Run a Moon Source procedural-projection audit.

Governing source or procedure:
[paste the source, doctrine or procedure description]

Candidate procedural projection:
[paste the skill, portable, mirror or handoff]

Return:
1. Responsibility preserved: what truth/state/authority still belongs to the governing source?
2. Procedural intelligence preserved: triggers, anti-triggers, steps, guards, outputs, QA and recovery.
3. Live state to remove: facts, dates, names, permissions or decisions that should remain in the source.
4. Gaps: missing triggers, stop lines, boundaries, edge conditions, outputs or QA.
5. Synchronization implications: doctrinal change, factual change, rename/version/deprecation or no action.
6. Smallest repair: the minimum change that restores the procedure without exposing private machinery.
7. Surface label: native capability, portable projection or fallback/mirror.
8. Attribution and lineage: origin, relationship, local adaptation and permission scope when applicable.
9. Uncertainty: what cannot be established from the supplied material.

Do not invent private compiler rules, thresholds, evaluation labels or adoption claims.
~~~
