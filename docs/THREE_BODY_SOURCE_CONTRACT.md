# Three-Body Source Contract

This is the public-safe contract for the relationship among the Moon Source bodies. It governs public interpretation and disclosure boundaries; it does not expose private corpus structure or grant access to any private source.

## Topology

| Body or surface | Authority and role | Not implied |
|---|---|---|
| **Moon Source** | Broader method and ecosystem identity | Not a fourth repository or a claim that every related source is public |
| **Moon Source Public** | Canonical public body, versioned in [luahelenammc/Moon-Source](https://github.com/luahelenammc/Moon-Source) main | Not the full private or professional corpus |
| **Local Moon Source** | Private Moon-specific operational laboratory; it may selectively inherit useful public capability and keep private overlays | Public access does not override local sovereignty |
| **Moon Professional Source** | Professional-operational body for skills, proof, offers, portfolio, outreach and public-facing translation | Not a premium edition or blind mirror of the public body |
| **Commercial / Enterprise Reserved** | Protected subtree inside MPS for explicitly reserved operational advantage, confidential delivery logic or restricted material | Not a fourth sovereign body and not a label for all MPS material |
| **/moonsource/ facade** | Human-facing convenience access to the public body | Not a competing semantic authority |
| **/ia/ surface** | Professional-facing surface governed by the professional source | Not evidence of client adoption |

## Cross-body delta fields

Use only the fields needed by the delta:

- home — sovereign source body;
- upstream / downstream — lineage and intended echo;
- public_status — local_only, public_candidate, public or deprecated;
- professional_status — none, professional_internal, professional_public or proof_candidate;
- commercial_status — none, public_service, reserved or confidential;
- sync_rule — inheritance, reference, candidate, sanitized transfer or no echo;
- promotion_gate / demotion_gate — what must be true before a status changes;
- authority / freshness — the source and currentness that govern the decision.

A small delta does not need every field. Missing detail is not permission to invent a route.

## Synchronization classes

### Class A — safe or near-automatic echo

Use for canonical URLs, public component rename/version/path, controlled terminology, bridge compatibility, already-authorized public claims and deprecation/source pointers. Echoing still requires a destination readback; it does not disclose private material.

### Class B — candidate echo

Use when a delta may be generally useful but needs ordinary source-owner metabolization, peer-utility review, claim checks, privacy/provenance review or destination-specific wording. A candidate is not a publication.

### Class C — explicit Moon authorization

Require explicit authority before crossing a public/private boundary or changing disclosure status. This includes protected heuristics, scoring, evaluation corpora, compiler/repair/reconciliation machinery, internal adapters, proprietary automation, client logic, private corpora, relicensing or external-adoption claims.

## Public-promotion doctrine

Generic, transferable capability tends toward the public body when it is stable, independently useful, proportionate, attributable, safe to disclose and reconstructable within the intended public scope. Operational advantage, intimate or situated material, confidential delivery logic and crown-jewel mechanisms remain reserved unless Moon explicitly authorizes a bounded disclosure.

Public inheritance is selective, not blind mirroring. Public method may be used as professional proof; professional interpretation does not rewrite the public body. Bridges carry context and provenance but never transfer authority.

## Non-claims

This contract does not claim automatic synchronization, universal adoption, measured impact, enterprise readiness, a private/public feature-equivalence guarantee or a future ChatGPT workbench as a public authority. Public repository history governs public repository state; private Drive history governs private and professional source state.
