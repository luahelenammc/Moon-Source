This document governs how the public Moon Source body records authorship, source lineage, adaptation, permission scope, mirroring and credit when material moves between sources, components, projects, people, models or public surfaces.

It is a public operational interface. It is not a repository-wide license, a legal opinion, an enforcement system or a claim that every external source has been independently verified.

## 1. Governing laws

Moon Source uses credits as part of context integrity:

- absorbing a resource does not erase its origin;
- adapting a method does not make the local re-expression the donor's original work;
- access, visibility or discussion does not create authority or permission;
- public or readable does not mean licensed;
- attribution does not imply endorsement, partnership or validation;
- a ledger entry records a relationship but does not make the candidate canonical;
- a local implementation may deserve its own authorship while preserving the donor lineage that materially shaped it.

`ATTRIBUTION_AND_LINEAGE.md` owns the public authorship and lineage statement. This protocol owns the repeatable operation for applying that statement across the public body.

## 2. Relationship classes

Use the narrowest description that the evidence supports:

| Relationship | Public meaning | Required care |
|---|---|---|
| **Original** | Created locally from the available evidence | Do not erase material third-party lineage if it exists |
| **Reference / inspiration** | An external work informed framing or direction | Do not call the result a copy or an official derivative without evidence |
| **Adaptation** | A source was deliberately reshaped for a new purpose or grammar | Identify the original, the changes and the local destination |
| **Operational derivation** | A local procedure or implementation was re-expressed from a donor concept | Separate donor influence from local implementation and evidence |
| **Mirror** | The same artifact is transported to another path or surface | Preserve exact bytes, canonical path and fingerprint when applicable |
| **Composite** | Several sources materially contribute to one output | Credit each material lineage and identify the local synthesis |
| **Pending / unclear** | The relationship or permission scope is unresolved | Keep it analytic or provisional; do not let it govern active truth |

These are provenance labels, not legal classifications. When a legal or contractual question matters, use the applicable permission or professional advice rather than inferring from a label.

## 3. Minimum attribution record

For an external resource, adaptation, mirror or composite output, record only what is relevant, but preserve these fields when they change the decision:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Where did the material come from? |
| **Author / community** | Who created it, or is authorship unknown? |
| **Source lineage** | Is there an upstream, mediator, fork, mirror or named artifact to preserve? |
| **Absorbed** | What was actually used? |
| **Adapted** | What changed in the local formulation? |
| **Rejected / contained** | What was deliberately not imported or kept out of the active source? |
| **Renamed / integrated** | Was the material given a new local name or destination? |
| **Relationship** | Original, inspiration, adaptation, operational derivation, mirror, composite or pending? |
| **Permission status** | What permission or usage basis is actually known? |
| **Permission scope** | What does that permission cover, and what does it not cover? |
| **Destination** | Which source, procedure, portable, registry, mirror or archive governs the result? |
| **Risk** | Is there a privacy, authorship, licence, factuality, security or bloat risk? |
| **Canonicalization status** | `analytic_only`, `pending`, `applied`, `mirrored` or `rejected`? |
| **State** | Active, incubating, superseded or archived? |

Recording a field as `unknown`, `pending` or `not established` is preferable to filling it with a confident fiction.

## 4. Credits operation

Run the smallest version of this operation that the material requires:

1. Identify the source artifact, current authority and relevant public path.
2. Separate original author, intermediary, upstream project and local re-expression.
3. Classify the relationship.
4. Record what was absorbed, adapted, refused, renamed or left analytic-only.
5. Check permission and its actual scope. Do not infer permission from public availability or silence.
6. Choose the local destination. External material does not become a governing source merely because it was useful.
7. Attach a compact credit with the original canonical reference, local adaptation identity and the professional context link.
8. If the result is an exact mirror, preserve the canonical bytes, path, version and fingerprint.
9. Recheck date-sensitive claims before publication or reuse.
10. Run the public QA below and retain uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.

The operation may end in `no_delta`, `analytic_only`, `adapted`, `mirrored`, `pending_permission` or `rejected`. A credit record can survive an analytical rejection; it must not silently turn rejection into incorporation.

## 6. Applying the protocol across Moon Source

The protocol is applied by responsibility, not by copying a long footer into every file:

| Public component | Application |
|---|---|
| **Repository and root docs** | Name Moon as creator and final authority, identify Áurion's bounded coauthorial role, keep the canonical repository visible and route reuse questions here. |
| **Architecture and method docs** | Preserve method lineage, distinguish local formulation from external influence and keep claim ceilings attached to the evidence. |
| **Public portables** | Carry creator, version, canonical path, public surface, professional context and adaptation guidance. The portable remains independently readable within its declared scope. |
| **Procedural projections and source-hygiene docs** | Treat missing provenance, unclear attribution or authority drift as bounded diagnostic findings; do not expose private ledgers, compilers or repair machinery. |
| **Registries and release records** | Preserve identity, version, canonical path, fingerprint, mirror path and attribution route without turning a documentation component into a portable. |
| **Website and raw-file mirrors** | Keep the repository as semantic authority, preserve exact bytes where promised and retain a visible path back to the canonical source. |
| **Handoffs, packets and generated outputs** | State which components were used, what was adapted and who performed the local synthesis. Do not launder source material into an unattributed generic output. |
| **Archives and legacy paths** | Preserve historical lineage while marking the material as non-governing unless a current source explicitly promotes it. |

This matrix is the integration contract for the current public component family. It does not claim that private Personal Skills, private source corpora or Citadel runtime internals are public Moon Source components.

## 7. Public QA gate

Before publishing, mirroring or teaching from a component, check:

- human creator and final authority are named where the component needs them;
- AI-assisted development is described without transferring human authorship or authority;
- the professional context link is present where a public credit is required: https://www.luahelena.com.br/ia/?lang=en;
- the canonical repository and component path are recoverable;
- material external lineage is identified rather than silently absorbed;
- adaptation, mirror or composite status is explicit;
- permission and its scope are not stronger than the evidence;
- no endorsement, partnership, adoption, impact or validation claim was smuggled into the credit;
- date-sensitive claims are marked for refresh;
- fingerprints and mirror state are checked when exact identity is promised;
- private, intimate, third-party or security-sensitive material remains outside the public surface;
- no broad licence is implied merely because the component is public.

If a check fails, the smallest repair is usually a provenance marker, canonical link, scope qualifier or mirror update. Do not compensate by publishing a private ledger or inventing a stronger claim.

## 8. Boundary and claim ceiling

This public component exposes the responsibility and bounded operation of credits and attribution. It does not publish:

- private ingestion ledgers or third-party confidential material;
- hidden permission-resolution heuristics or legal decision machinery;
- private source-authority rules, compiler logic or repair ordering;
- a repository-wide open-source licence;
- evidence of external adoption, impact, endorsement or universal validity.

The method is public and inspectable. Its use in a given output still depends on the governing source, the component's own terms and any applicable permission.
