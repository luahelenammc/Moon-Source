Credits & Attribution Ops is a reusable meta-operational module for preserving authorship, provenance, adaptation history, permission scope and destination when material moves between sources, projects, people, models or public surfaces.

It is intentionally universal. It can be applied inside Moon Source or transplanted into another source-governed project without making that project subordinate to Moon Source.

This module does not decide how Moon Source itself may be used. That authority lives in [Moon Source Use & Attribution](../MOON_SOURCE_USE_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md).

## 1. Governing laws

- Absorbing a resource does not erase its origin.
- Adapting a method does not make the local re-expression the donor's original work.
- Access, visibility, discussion or availability do not create authority or permission.
- Attribution does not imply endorsement, partnership, validation or adoption.
- A provenance record does not make the recorded material canonical.
- A local implementation may deserve its own authorship while preserving the donor lineage that materially shaped it.
- Unknown permission, origin or authorship should remain unknown rather than being filled by confident fiction.

## 2. Relationship classes

Use the narrowest description supported by evidence.

| Relationship | Operational meaning | Required care |
|---|---|---|
| **Original** | Created locally from the available evidence | Do not erase material donor lineage if one exists |
| **Reference / inspiration** | An external work informed framing or direction | Do not imply copying, endorsement or official derivation |
| **Adaptation** | A source was deliberately reshaped for a new purpose or grammar | Identify the origin, material changes and local destination |
| **Operational derivation** | A procedure or implementation was re-expressed from a donor concept | Separate donor influence from local implementation and evidence |
| **Mirror** | The same artifact is transported to another path or surface | Preserve canonical path and fingerprint/version when exact identity matters |
| **Composite** | Several sources materially contribute to one output | Credit each material lineage and identify the local synthesis |
| **Pending / unclear** | Relationship, authorship or permission scope is unresolved | Keep it provisional and prevent silent canonicalization |

These are provenance labels, not legal classifications.

## 3. Minimum attribution record

Record only what changes the decision, but preserve these fields when relevant:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Where did the material come from? |
| **Author / community** | Who created it, or is authorship unknown? |
| **Source lineage** | Is there an upstream, mediator, fork, mirror or named artifact? |
| **Absorbed** | What was actually used? |
| **Adapted** | What changed locally? |
| **Rejected / contained** | What was deliberately not imported? |
| **Renamed / integrated** | Was the material renamed or moved into another local form? |
| **Relationship** | Original, inspiration, adaptation, derivation, mirror, composite or pending? |
| **Permission status** | What usage basis is actually known? |
| **Permission scope** | What does it cover, and what remains outside it? |
| **Destination** | Which source, procedure, registry, mirror or archive governs the result? |
| **Risk** | Is there privacy, authorship, licence, factuality, security or bloat risk? |
| **Canonicalization status** | `analytic_only`, `pending`, `applied`, `mirrored` or `rejected`? |
| **State** | Active, incubating, superseded or archived? |

## 4. Attribution operation

Run the smallest version the material requires:

1. Identify the source artifact and current authority.
2. Separate original author, intermediary, upstream project and local re-expression.
3. Classify the relationship.
4. Record what was absorbed, adapted, refused, renamed or left analytic-only.
5. Check permission and its actual scope. Do not infer permission from availability or silence.
6. Choose the local destination. Useful material does not become a governing source automatically.
7. Attach a proportional credit that preserves origin and local adaptation identity.
8. For exact mirrors, preserve canonical path, version and fingerprint when applicable.
9. Recheck date-sensitive claims before publication or reuse.
10. Run the QA gate and retain uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.

Valid outcomes include `no_delta`, `analytic_only`, `adapted`, `mirrored`, `pending_permission` and `rejected`.

A credit record may survive rejection. Rejection must not silently become incorporation later.

## 5. Copyable generic patterns

### Reference / inspiration

> Informed by **[source]** by **[author/community]**. Local interpretation by **[person/project]**, **[date/version]**. Canonical source: **[path/URL]**.

### Adaptation

> Adapted from **[source]** by **[author/community]**. Local modifications by **[person/project]**, **[date/version]**. Original: **[path/URL]**. Preserve the original lineage and applicable terms.

### Mirror

> Canonical artifact: **[path/URL]**. This copy is a convenience mirror. Verify version and fingerprint before treating exact byte identity as current.

### Composite

> Based on **[material sources]**, with local synthesis by **[person/project]**, **[date/version]**. Preserve each material lineage; no endorsement or partnership is implied.

The patterns preserve provenance. They do not grant permission.

## 6. Integration contract

When transplanted into another project, this module should remain subordinate to that project's own authority and terms.

A project using Credits & Attribution Ops should decide locally:

- where provenance records live;
- which artifacts require visible credits;
- what counts as material influence;
- who can promote a record from pending to applied;
- what permission authority controls reuse;
- which private ledgers must remain private;
- how mirrors and fingerprints are validated;
- when an attribution record is archived.

Do not import Moon Source-specific identity, links, ownership or public-surface rules as if they governed the receiving project.

## 7. QA gate

Before publishing, mirroring, adapting or teaching from material, check:

- origin and author are named where known and material;
- local adaptation is distinguished from donor work;
- external lineage has not been silently absorbed;
- permission and its scope are no stronger than the evidence;
- no endorsement, partnership, adoption, impact or validation claim was smuggled into the credit;
- canonical source and destination are recoverable;
- date-sensitive claims are refreshed when needed;
- fingerprints and mirror state are checked when exact identity is promised;
- private, confidential or security-sensitive material remains outside public credits;
- uncertainty remains explicit where evidence is incomplete.

## 8. Boundary

This module is not:

- a repository-wide licence;
- legal advice;
- an enforcement mechanism;
- proof that a donor authorized a use;
- proof of external adoption or endorsement;
- a substitute for the receiving project's source authority;
- permission to expose private attribution ledgers or confidential material.
